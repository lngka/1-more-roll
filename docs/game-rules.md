# Dice (Farkle) — Game Rules

## Objective

Two players take turns rolling dice. The first player to **reach or exceed the target score** wins.

The target is agreed before the match (typical values: 1500 or 4000).

## Setup

- Each player uses **six** standard six-sided dice.
- Some dice may show a **Devil’s Head** instead of a pip face. Treat it as a joker (see below). A Devil’s Head is optional flavor, not required for a legal game.

## Turn flow

1. A turn starts by rolling **all 6 dice**.
2. After a roll, the player **must set aside at least one scoring combination** from that roll.
3. Then choose:
   - **Bank (score and pass):** add this turn’s points to the player’s permanent total and end the turn.
   - **Continue (score and continue):** keep the set-aside dice, reroll the remaining dice, and try to add more points.
4. If a roll has **no scoring combination**, the player **busts**: all points from the current turn are lost, and the opponent’s turn begins.
5. If the player scores with **every remaining die** (all 6 used across the turn), they **hot-dice**: roll all 6 again and keep accumulating on the same turn.
6. Combinations are scored **only from the current roll**. Dice already banked earlier in the turn do not combine with new dice.

The player may set aside **only part** of what scores (for example one 5 instead of two) in order to reroll more dice.

## Scoring

Multiple combinations from the **same roll** add together.

| Combination | Points |
|---|---|
| Single 1 | 100 |
| Single 5 | 50 |
| Straight 1–5 | 500 |
| Straight 2–6 | 750 |
| Straight 1–6 | 1500 |
| Three 1s | 1000 |
| Three 2s | 200 |
| Three 3s | 300 |
| Three 4s | 400 |
| Three 5s | 500 |
| Three 6s | 600 |

Singles of 2, 3, 4, or 6 **do not score** unless they are part of a straight or three-of-a-kind (or better).

### Extra matching dice

After a three-of-a-kind, **each additional die of the same face doubles** that combination’s value:

| Face | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1000 | 2000 | 4000 | 8000 |
| 2 | 200 | 400 | 800 | 1600 |
| 3 | 300 | 600 | 1200 | 2400 |
| 4 | 400 | 800 | 1600 | 3200 |
| 5 | 500 | 1000 | 2000 | 4000 |
| 6 | 600 | 1200 | 2400 | 4800 |

Example: `1, 4, 4, 4, 5` scores 100 + 400 + 50 = **550**.

## Devil’s Head

A Devil’s Head is a **joker**. It takes the face needed to complete the best combination. It has **no points by itself**.

Examples:

- Two 3s + Devil’s Head → three 3s (300).
- `1, 2, 3, 4, 5` + Devil’s Head → straight 1–6 (1500).

## Busts and banking

- Unbanked turn points are at risk until the player banks.
- Banked points cannot be lost on later turns.
- After a bust, only that turn’s unbanked points are lost.
